Who we are

The Ghana side of a 20-year-old tea factory.

We represent Deyuntang Health in Ghana. They have been making tea products for over 20 years. The product comes straight from their production line to our warehouse in Accra — no middlemen between you and the maker.

We keep stock locally so shops, clinics and online sellers can restock in days instead of months, and we deliver to every region in Ghana. Because we work directly with the factory, we can also put your brand on the box.
